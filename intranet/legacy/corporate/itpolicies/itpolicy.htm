TLD GROUP IT Policy

Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define common IT rules and procedures that must be followed by all within the whole TLD GROUP in order to insure:

reliable connectivity and data exchange between all users

optimal data security and confidentiality

There may be some special cases where Group Connectivity and Security rules conflict with Local Connectivity and Security rules. In these situations, compromises may be taken but the final decision is to be taken by the TLD Steering committee.

The TLD Steering committee has given the TLD GROUP IT Coordinator the effective responsibility for proposing, auditing and controlling all subjects in the IT field. All IT local managers shall communicate transparently and diligently with the TLD GROUP IT Coordinator for all IT related subjects. Any network modification should always be submitted for approval to the TLD GROUP IT Coordinator before implementation.

It is the responsibility of the local management to insure that these rules are followed and respected in each of their locations. It is also their responsibility to insure that the relevant local IT procedures meet the local laws and the IT Group policy and are effectively written.

Network:

The Group network is divided into three major WANs (Wide Area Network): TLD Europe, TLD Asia and TLD USA. Each WAN is comprised of LANs (Local Area Networks) in all major facility locations. All three WANs are linked together using VPN connections to form the TLD GROUP WAN. TLD WAN coordination and connectivity is insured by the TLD GROUP IT Coordinator.
