TLD News Handheld Version

TLD News is now available in a handheld version for users with handheld devices such as PalmOS, WinCE, etc. If you do not have Avantgo installed yet go here to sign up for free service and download latest software version. After installing the software click here to add the TLD Handheld Version channel to your Avantgo. The first time you sync your handheld and access the TLD channel it will request your TLD Website userid and password i.e. the username/password combination you used to access this Internal Homepage. The second time you sync the content will be downloaded. Password request will only happen once unless you delete your password from your handheld.

Only the internal news will appear on your handheld for now. More content will be added as the system is developed.

Please contact me if you have any problems with this service.
